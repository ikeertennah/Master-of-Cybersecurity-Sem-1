# MECR1023 – Information Security Governance & Risk Management

**Semester:** 1 | **Year:** 2025/2026 | **Instructor:** Assoc. Prof. Dr. Norafida Ithnin

---

## 🎯 Learning Objectives

- Apply qualitative and quantitative risk assessment frameworks (Likelihood x Impact matrices) to prioritize enterprise security risks.
- Develop comprehensive security policies (Identity Management, Access Control) aligned with business strategy and regulatory requirements.
- Execute project management techniques including Work Breakdown Structure (WBS), PERT analysis, and Critical Path Method (CPM) for security initiative planning.
- Design and implement Security Education, Training, and Awareness (SETA) programs to mitigate human risk factors (phishing, password sharing).
- Align security strategy with business goals using strategic planning tools (SWOT, PESTLE, Vision/Mission statements).
- Evaluate and recommend security controls (MFA, PAM, JIT Access) based on cost-benefit and risk reduction analysis.

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Frameworks Used

- **PERT / CPM Analysis** – Calculated expected task durations and identified critical paths for security project scheduling.
- **Risk Assessment Matrices** – Built heat maps to score risks (Likelihood x Impact) and prioritize mitigation efforts (e.g., Phishing scored 25/25).
- **SANS Policy Templates** – Referenced to structure Identity and Access Management policies.
- **SWOT & PESTLE Analysis** – Conducted environmental scanning for strategic planning at National Oilwell Varco (NOV).
- **Security Awareness Platforms** – Designed phishing simulation workflows and embedded coaching mechanisms.
- **Privileged Access Management (PAM)** – Conceptualized Just-In-Time (JIT) access to eliminate static credential sharing.

---

## 📚 Key Skills Developed

- **Information Security Governance** – Defined security strategy, risk appetite, and oversight structures.
- **Risk Assessment & Prioritization** – Identified, scored, and justified mitigation of high-impact risks (Phishing, Shadow IT, Password Sharing).
- **Security Policy Writing** – Drafted detailed Identity Management Policies for a global energy company.
- **Strategic & Operational Planning** – Developed 3-year roadmaps linking security to business growth.
- **SETA Program Design** – Created posters, micro-training videos, and security champion programs.
- **Project Management** – Decomposed projects using WBS and optimized timelines using CPM.

## 🔍 Reflection

This course transformed my understanding of security from a purely technical discipline to a strategic business enabler. The most valuable takeaway was learning to speak the language of business justifying security investments through risk quantification rather than fear. Building a full strategic plan for a real-world company (NOV) taught me how to balance legacy operations with emerging threats and energy transition opportunities. I also gained hands-on experience in project management (PERT/CPM), which I now see as essential for delivering security initiatives on time. The biggest challenge was distinguishing between Identity and Access Management policies—but mastering this distinction is critical for implementing least-privilege and segregation of duties in any enterprise. I plan to apply these governance and planning frameworks in my future roles to bridge the gap between technical teams and executive leadership.
